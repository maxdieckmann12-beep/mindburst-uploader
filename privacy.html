2. Summary

MindBurst Uploader is a personal tool that runs locally on the operator's own computer. It uploads videos to the operator's own TikTok account. It does not run a server, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not sell or share data with anyone other than TikTok.

3. Data we process

  • TikTok account information (scope user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar. These are used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after login. These are needed to upload videos on your behalf.
  • Account and video statistics (scopes user.info.stats and video.list): follower, like and video counts of the account, and the list of its public videos with view, like, comment and share counts. These are used only to evaluate which of the operator’s own videos perform well, and are stored only locally.
  • Videos and captions you choose to upload (scopes video.upload and video.publish), plus the post settings you select (for example privacy level or AI-generated label).

We do not collect passwords. Login happens directly on TikTok's own website.

4. Purpose and legal basis

The data is processed only to upload your videos to your TikTok account and to evaluate the performance of your own videos. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the service you requested) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent when you grant the permissions on TikTok).

5. Where data is stored

Tokens and account information are stored only in a local file on the operator's computer. Videos and captions are sent directly to TikTok's servers via the official TikTok API. No other servers are involved.

6. Recipients

The only recipient is TikTok (TikTok Technology Limited, Ireland, and its affiliates), which processes the uploaded content according to the TikTok Privacy Policy. We do not pass data to any other third party.

7. Retention

Tokens are kept only as long as the app is connected to your TikTok account. They are deleted when you disconnect the app or delete the local token file. Uploaded videos remain on TikTok until you delete them there.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time. You can revoke the app's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children

The app is not intended for people under 18.
