1. About the app

MindBurst Uploader ("the app") is a small personal tool operated by Max Dieckmann ("we", "us"). It uploads short videos created for the TikTok channel MindBurst to the operator's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. The app is not offered to the public and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance.

2. Who may use the app

The app is intended solely for use by the operator with the operator's own TikTok account. By connecting a TikTok account, you confirm that you own the account or are authorized to manage it.

3. What the app does

  • It asks you to log in with TikTok and to grant the permissions user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list, video.upload and video.publish.
  • It reads the statistics of your own account and public videos (followers, views, likes, comments, shares) so you can see which videos perform well.
  • It uploads video files you choose, together with the caption and settings you choose, to your TikTok account, either as a draft or as a direct post.
  • It does not follow, like, comment, message or interact with other accounts, and it does not generate followers, views or any other engagement.
